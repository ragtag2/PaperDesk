# Paperdesk

Paperdesk coordinates internal support requests and incidents by identifying relevant teams and people.

## Language

**Ticket**:
A report of an issue or request whose current facts provide the basis for coordination.

**Coordination**:
An assessment of a ticket containing a summary, relevant teams with reasons for their involvement, and stakeholders.

**Relevant team**:
A team supported by current ticket evidence because it owns work needed to handle the issue or its business work is clearly affected.

**Responsible team**:
A team that owns work needed to handle the ticket's issue.
_Avoid_: Resolving team.

**Affected team**:
A team whose business work is clearly affected by the issue described in the current ticket.

**Stakeholder**:
An active member of a relevant team included in a ticket's coordination. The coordination includes every active member of its relevant teams.
